BrainPlay

Articles

Three short articles on what research does and does not say about games, playing together and daily routines. Each one names its sources and states its limits.

Please note: BrainPlay is for entertainment and practice. It has not been shown to prevent or treat dementia and is not a medical assessment.
  • What can brain games really do?Targeted practice, what long-term trials found, and why a promising 2026 result is not proof that ordinary games prevent dementia.
  • Make a game an invitationPlaying with someone as an occasion to talk, and what the small-group research can and cannot tell us.
  • A game is one part of your dayEnjoyment, a comfortable pace and the wider picture of healthy routines, and why a game score is not a diagnosis.

How to read them

These articles are about related research. None of the studies they cite tested BrainPlay. Sources were checked on 2 November 2026; two of the studies were read as abstracts only, and each article says so where it matters. The articles are in English only.
