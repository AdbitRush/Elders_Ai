The short version

  • You never need an account, an email address or a password to play.
  • Your scores, levels, name and settings stay in your own browser. They are not sent to us.

We ask for consent to advertising cookies and personalised advertising where required by law.
  • You can change your choice at any time using Cookie settings on this page or in the games homepage footer.

What is kept on your device

To remember your progress, BrainPlay saves a small amount of information in your browser's own storage on this device: your levels and best scores, the name and picture you chose (if you typed one), your favourite games, badges, the daily challenge, and your settings (language, light or dark display, text size, sound and difficulty).

BrainPlay stores this game information in your browser on this device and does not send it to our servers. Anyone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to see it. Progress is not automatically transferred to another device. To delete it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Game progress and settings remain in your browser until you clear this site's data, your browser removes it, or you reset them in the site.

Cookies

  • Your cookie choice (needed for the site): one cookie remembers whether you accepted or refused advertising cookies, so we do not ask on every visit. It lasts about six months.

Advertising through Google AdSense: When advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google and participating advertising partners, may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads and help prevent fraud. With the required consent, advertising cookies may be used to personalise ads based on visits to this site and other websites. You can accept or reject optional advertising cookies in the consent banner and change your choice in Cookie settings. Rejecting personalisation does not mean that all advertising stops collecting data, and non-personalised ads can still use cookies. Any cookies requiring consent must remain off unless you agree.

How Google uses information from sites that show its ads: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. To manage the ads Google shows you: Google Ads Settings.

Other services the pages use

  • Our server: when you open the site, your browser sends technical information, including your IP address, to the server hosting BrainPlay so it can deliver the pages.
  • Google Fonts: the lettering on the main page is loaded from Google's font service, which receives your device's internet address when it sends the font.
  • Flag pictures: the Flag Quiz loads flag images from flagcdn.com, which receives your device's internet address when it sends a picture.
  • Sharing: when you press a share button, the message opens in WhatsApp, email or your phone's own share menu. Nothing is shared unless you press it.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent information about you.
  • We do not ask for your email, phone number, address or date of birth.

If you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include so we can respond to your request. Please do not send passwords, payment information or sensitive health information.

Who the site is for

BrainPlay is made for adults, especially people aged 50 and over. It is not directed at children.

Your rights

You can delete locally stored game information by clearing this site's data in your browser. You can change or withdraw your advertising consent at any time through Cookie settings; withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before you withdrew it. If EU or UK data-protection law applies, you may also have rights to access, correct or erase personal data, restrict or object to its processing, and receive a portable copy, where applicable. To make a request, use the contact email under 'Who runs this site'. These rights apply to personal data we process; information stored only in your browser is managed on your device. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes
