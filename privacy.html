Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Who runs this site

BrainPlay is the operator of this website. You can reach us at [CONTACT EMAIL — to be added before launch].

The short version

  • You never need an account, an email address or a password to play.
  • Your scores, levels, name and settings stay in your own browser. They are not sent to us.
  • BrainPlay is free because small ads from Google are shown between games, never during a game. Google may use cookies for those ads only if you say yes.
  • You can change your answer at any time with Cookie settings (also at the bottom of every page).

What is kept on your device

To remember your progress, BrainPlay saves a small amount of information in your browser's own storage on this device: your levels and best scores, the name and picture you chose (if you typed one), your favourite games, badges, the daily challenge, and your settings (language, light or dark display, text size, sound and difficulty).

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and nobody else can. If you use another phone or computer, it starts fresh there. To delete it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Cookies

  • Your cookie choice (needed for the site): one cookie remembers whether you accepted or refused advertising cookies, so we do not ask on every visit. It lasts about six months.
  • Advertising cookies (only with your permission): Google may set cookies to measure ads and show ones that suit you. If you refuse, Google may still show ads, but not ones based on your browsing.

How Google uses information from sites that show its ads: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Other services the pages use

  • Google Fonts: the lettering on the main page is loaded from Google's font service, which receives your device's internet address when it sends the font.
  • Flag pictures: the Flag Quiz loads flag images from flagcdn.com, which receives your device's internet address when it sends a picture.
  • Sharing: when you press a share button, the message opens in WhatsApp, email or your phone's own share menu. Nothing is shared unless you press it.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent information about you.
  • We do not ask for your email, phone number, address or date of birth.
  • The web server that hosts BrainPlay is not set up to keep a log of visits.

Who the site is for

BrainPlay is made for adults, especially people aged 50 and over. It is not directed at children.

Your rights

Because your game information stays on your own device, you are in control of it: you can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data. You can withdraw or give consent to advertising cookies at any time with Cookie settings. For any question about privacy, write to [CONTACT EMAIL — to be added before launch]. If you live in the EU or the UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
